## 💡 Facebook Posting Best Practices & Pro Tips

1. **The 3-Second Rule (The Hook):**  
   Facebook truncates posts after 3 to 5 lines with a `...See more` button. The hook must create enough curiosity or emotional tension that clicking `...See more` is irresistible.

2. **The "First Comment" Strategy:**  
   If posting organically on your personal profile or page, do NOT put the external URL in the main post text. Facebook's algorithm penalizes outbound links to keep users on platform. Instead, use:  
   *"Link is pinned in the first comment! 👇"* OR ask for a keyword comment.

3. **Comment Auto-Responder Trick:**  
   When people comment keywords like *"BLUEPRINT"* or *"FAST"*, reply to their comment with enthusiasm and send the link via Messenger. This signals the Facebook algorithm that your post is generating high two-way conversational engagement, pushing it to 5x more friends and group members.

4. **Pair with Clean High-Contrast Images:**  
   Posts with 1:1 high-contrast graphics (orange `#F26B21` and dark card `#14141A`) stop the mobile thumb-scroll immediately. Use the corresponding high-res graphics generated in `promotional_assets/images/`.
